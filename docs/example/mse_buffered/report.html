Scope and assumptions

Four fitted assessment cases supply the simulated stocks. Each rule is tested for 15 years under two growth scenarios, with 20 repeated trials per case and scenario. The same random errors are used when comparing rules.

Each year, an index is observed with error, a catch rule is applied, and the stock responds to that catch. The next observation comes from the updated stock. The index-based rules use a three-year mean; they do not see the true stock size.

Intrinsic growth stays at its baseline mean, or uses 0.7 of that mean for six years before recovering. Year-to-year growth still varies in both scenarios.

Fixed growth sensitivities and initial biomass; fitted cases are not weighted probabilities. Growth and observation errors are declared toy lognormal perturbations. No assessment is refitted during these management trials. Empirical trial fractions are conditional illustrations, not validated risk estimates. Annual growth is capped at one to retain the simple Euler update within its stable range.
