Source projection · Annual catch · Selection SQL

The SQL selects six cell–quarter strata observed in every year. CPUE A uses year and stratum effects; CPUE B uses year and quarter. Predictions average these six strata equally, in fish per 1,000 hooks; each relative index starts at one.

HHOOKS is hundreds of hooks (multiplied by 100); ALB_N counts fish. Masked catch/effort cells are excluded; their zeros are not observed zero catches. Annual South Pacific stock catch is a separate all-fleet, all-gear series in metric tonnes.

The small Fiji index is not validated as stock-wide abundance. Simplified assessments and management trials provide no stock advice. WCPFC/SPC notices and attribution remain with the data; the code licence does not relicense them.

-- Published aggregates, not individual fishing sets. Keep the fixed observed support.
SELECT set_id, source_row, year, fleet, quarter, cell, stratum, hooks, catch_n
FROM sets
WHERE fleet = 'FJ' AND hooks > 0 AND catch_n >= 0
  AND stratum IN ('20S_170E_Q4', '20S_175E_Q1', '20S_175E_Q2',
                  '20S_175E_Q3', '20S_175E_Q4', '25S_175E_Q3')
ORDER BY year, cell, quarter, source_row;
