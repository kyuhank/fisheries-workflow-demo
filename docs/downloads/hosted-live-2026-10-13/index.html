Sources and conditions

The example uses WCPFC/SPC public longline catch and effort and South Pacific annual catch estimates. Effort is converted from hundreds of hooks to hooks; albacore catch numbers feed the CPUE models, while the separate annual catch series is in metric tonnes. The relative CPUE index is dimensionless. Confidentiality-masked zeros are excluded.

Retain WCPFC/SPC attribution and source notices. The code's MIT licence does not relicense the statistical data. See the pinned public-data guide and the notices preserved inside each bundle.
